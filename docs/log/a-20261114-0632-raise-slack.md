# Lower late raise, longer minimum slack: within noise

**What we saw.** Raises cost about €900 a week (8% of orders raised to 1.8x), and gigs posted with under 8 hours to their last pickup miss half the time.

**The ideas.** `driver.fee_raise` 1.5 instead of 1.8; `driver.min_slack` 360 instead of 240.

**The test.** rc7, rc8, rc9 (36 weeks), paired against rc5 defaults.

| Tag | Ranked (36) | Min on-time | Worst three weeks | p10 | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rc5 | 35 | 84.4% | -€345, €1,627, €2,503 | €3,037 | €6,254 |
| fee_raise 1.5 | 35 | 82.5% | -€784, €1,700, €2,569 | €3,204 | €6,360 |
| min_slack 360 | 35 | 83.7% | -€318, €1,627, €2,290 | €3,056 | €6,234 |

fee_raise 1.5 gains on 26 of 36 weeks (+€106 mean) but makes the worst week worse (rc9-05, which fails in every variant).

**Keep or kill.** Kill both under our rule (no worse worst week, gain larger than the spread). fee_raise 1.5 is the closest call so far; noted.
