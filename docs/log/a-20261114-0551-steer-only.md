# Same_day extra only when there is a slower option: no gain

**What we saw.** About 31% of requests offer only same_day, and we win almost none. The same_day extra (+0.2 on the ratio) exists to steer dealers that also accept next_day; on a same_day-only request it only makes us lose. When the dealer accepts both and picks next_day we earn €39 an order; next_day-only requests earn €25. same_day lists about 8% above next_day.

**The idea.** `dealer.steer_only`: add the extra only when the request also offers a slower option.

**The test.** rc7-01..12 and rc8-01..12, paired against rc4 defaults.

| Tag | Ranked | Min on-time | p10 | Median | Mean delta | Orders a week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rc7-base | 11/12 | 82.0% | €3,589 | €8,341 | | 240 |
| rc7 steer_only | 11/12 | 82.0% | €3,603 | €7,576 | -€195 | 240 |
| rc8-base | 12/12 | 92.0% | €3,719 | €5,763 | | 194 |
| rc8 steer_only | 12/12 | 91.2% | €3,420 | €5,976 | +€56 | 200 |

**Keep or kill.** Kill: within noise. Most same_day-only requests are declined anyway by the 4-hour minimum slack, so few more are won.
