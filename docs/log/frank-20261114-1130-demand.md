# V2 Demand: forecast of the cars we will still win (a5)

**What we saw.** The planner's `_future()` guessed future wins as 21 requests an hour from 08 to 18, split by a supply-based
region share, half landing on the arrival day and half on the next. Replayed on the 12 c3 weeks it misses the future won
cars per (region, deadline day) by 179 cars on average (of 170), and the week total by 61.

**The idea.** `src/spreadbot/demand.py`: requests still to arrive from a prior by (day, hour) and pickup place, times won
cars per request (prior by day and half-day x place factor) x this week's win scale, spread over deadline days and options by
the prior mix of won cars, re-weighted by this week's option shares. Regions come from `desk.supply.region()`, so any map works.

**The test.** 57 distinct weeks (a seed's request stream is identical in every run; one week per seed), leave one seed out,
checkpoints Mon-Fri 08/12/16 (`research/sessionA/scripts/a5_demand_check.py`, `a5_wins_check.py`); module replay
`lab/a/demand_check.py` on c3 (dev-07..18) and k18 (dev-31..42, another policy, ~400 orders/wk); unit tests `tests/a/test_demand.py`.

**The numbers.** Requests (today's rest, sum over regions |err|, mean actual 132): prior 41.5, flat stub 42.3; scaling a region
by what it sent so far is worse at every strength (A=10: 45.3 ... A=300: 41.8): batches make region counts clumpy (var/mean 2-7)
and nothing persists (morning/afternoon correlation -0.14..0.06; weekly totals 1,030-1,040 every week).
Future won cars per (region, deadline day), held out: c3 prior 72.6 vs stub 183.4; cross-policy (priors from c3, tested on
money / k18 / c1 weeks) 73.2 / 83.5 / 71.4 vs stub 194.5 / 207.5 / 164.8. A strong online win scale was worse (k18: K=30 94.3,
K=300 85.4 vs prior 83.5), so the scale has a weak weight (300 requests). Module replay: c3 77.6 (old rule 179.1), week
total 32.8 (61.1); k18 88.3 (207.4), total 36.5 (59.3).

**Keep or kill.** Keep, behind `v2` (a1 wires it into `Planner._future`). Caveat: the win part is c3's; V2 wins more and
the weak scale needs ~300 requests (Tue) to move halfway. If V2's win rate differs a lot, a1 may set `WIN_K` lower.
