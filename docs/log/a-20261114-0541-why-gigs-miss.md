# Why gigs miss their last pickup: windows, not fees

**What we saw.** Over 24 fresh weeks (rc7, rc8, rc4 defaults), share of gigs not booked before their order's `lastPickupAt`:

| Segment | Gigs | Not booked in time |
|---|---|---|
| slack at posting over 2 days | 2,484 | 1.3% |
| 1-2 days | 2,392 | 6.0% |
| 8-24 hours | 286 | 7.3% |
| under 8 hours | 529 | 51.2% |

Of the gigs posted with under 8 hours left: first posts of same_day orders book in time 246 of 279; re-posts almost never do (next_day 3 of 163, same_day 9 of 85). The fee does not explain it: 4.3% miss at about 1.0x the typical fee, 4.4% at about 1.4x.

Trouble towns (decline pickups after 1-2 deadline warnings in a town within two days) changed nothing: rc7-06 stayed at 81.8%; on rc8 the mean fell €310-478. Early booking speed does not flag the bad week either (rc7-06: 78% of gigs posted by Wednesday noon booked within 2 hours, like healthy weeks).

**The idea.** We post stops without windows, so the world uses the order's window cut to the site's stated hours: the pickup window closes with the site on the ready day, the gig expires, and our re-post goes out with no windows, so a driver may book it and come after `lastPickupAt`. Post explicit windows instead: pickup from the ready time to `lastPickupAt`, delivery to the deadline, on first posts and re-posts alike, while an on-time delivery is still possible.

**The test.** rc7-01..12 and rc8-01..12, paired, flag `driver.explicit_windows`.
