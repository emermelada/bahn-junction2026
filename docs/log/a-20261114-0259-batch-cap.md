# A cap per batch fixes the fresh-seed failures

**What we saw.** On 12 fresh seeds (rc4) the rc3 defaults were ranked 10 of 12. rc4-11 (86.6%) took 19 cars from one batch (B0045, a yard sending 23 requests) and 8 were late; rc4-01 (89.9%) took 11-13 cars from each of several batches.

**The idea.** Decline a request once we hold `max_per_batch` orders from its batchId. HANDBOOK: a dealer group can send 30 to 40 cars from one yard; with few drivers near that yard, each extra car is likely late.

**The test.** rc4-01..12, paired against rc4-base.

| Tag | Ranked | Min on-time | Mean delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| rc4-base (rc3) | 10/12 | 86.6% | |
| max_per_batch 6 | 12/12 | 93.6% | +€165 |
| max_per_batch 10 | 10/11 (stopped at 11) | 88.3% | +€144 |

**Keep or kill.** Keep 6, pending a check on fresh seeds (rc5) before it becomes the default. The town cap test (rc4-tc02) did not run: paused by request.
