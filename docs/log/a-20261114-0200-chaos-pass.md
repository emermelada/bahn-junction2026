# Chaos test: killed twice mid-week, still ranked

**What we saw.** The final has no `advance`: the clock runs by itself, 4.5 real seconds per request, and the bot must survive its own crash and a server restart.

**The idea.** The live runner rebuilds State from `world.snapshot()` (gigs, orders) and `world.events()` (everything else) on every start, then follows the stream. Dispatch is a reconciler over State, so after a restart it posts only what is missing.

**The test.** `harness/chaos.py`: a level 8 practice world from Thursday 00:00, advanced by a ticker at 4x the final's speed; the live runner in a subprocess, killed with SIGKILL after 90 s and again after 180 s, restarted 5 s later each time. A watcher polls the world every 5 s for any leg sitting on two live gigs.

Result: rejoined at minute 6148 with 30 orders and 31 gigs, and at minute 7272 with 51 orders and 54 gigs. The week ended ranked: 50 orders, 96% on time, +€1,184 (Thursday to the end only), 0 legs on two live gigs. Earlier emulator run at 8x: every refused action was a quote after its request closed (8x leaves 0.6 s per request).

**Keep or kill.** Keep. Still to do: an emulated week at 1x speed, and the test finals on Saturday at 14:00 and 20:00.
