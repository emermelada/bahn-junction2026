# Chains version 2 (no holding): still no gain

**What we saw.** Drivers are the limit; a two-car chain moves two cars with one driver-day. Version 1 held new cars up to 2 hours to find a partner and lost on-time share.

**The idea.** Hold nothing. Pair each new single car with another new car, or with a single still open, unbooked, never repriced and posted under 3 hours ago (withdraw it and post both as one gig). A pair needs A's delivery within 45 road minutes of B's pickup, both deadlines with an hour to spare, and at most 12 hours of driving. Chain fee: 0.9x or 1.0x the two single fees.

**The test.** Paired against rc4 defaults.

**Keep or kill.** Kill. Only about 20 chains form a week (the time windows and the 45-minute reach rarely line up), too few to add capacity, and they cost on-time share on rc7.
