# Outage and price-war detectors, loose governor: within noise

**What we saw.** Every level 8 week has an outage day (competitor down in one region, dealers there pay a backup 30% dearer) and a price-war day (competitor 20% cheaper everywhere). We reacted to neither. Many weeks end 96-99% on time, leaving lateness budget unused.

**The ideas.**
- Outage: if today we win 85% or more of 4+ quotes picked up within 80 km of a town, add 0.25 to the ratio there.
- Price war: if today's win rate (20+ quotes) falls under 40% of the week's so far, take 0.15 off the ratio for the day.
- Loose governor: Monday to Wednesday, while the projected late share is under 2%, allow a 1.6x larger backlog.

**The test.** 12 fresh seeds rc7-01..12, paired against rc4 defaults.

| Tag | Ranked | Min on-time | p10 | Median | Mean delta |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rc7-base (rc4) | 11/12 | 82.0% | €3,589 | €8,341 | |
| outage + war | 10/12 | 83.0% | €3,955 | €7,569 | +€61 |
| outage only | 11/12 | 83.0% | €3,589 | €8,228 | +€109 |
| loose governor | 11/12 | 81.5% | €3,589 | €8,214 | +€7 |

**Keep or kill.** Kill all three: no gain beyond the spread between seeds. The finding that matters is rc7-06: the rc4 defaults reach only 82.0% on time in a week with a deep driver shortage (168 orders, 30 late, spread over small towns: Kokkola 5 late of 7, Oulu 4 of 14). With rc4-base 10/12, rc5 12/12, rc6 11/12 and rc7 11/12, rc4 misses the gate in about 1 week in 12. Next: stop winning in a town once deadline warnings pile up there (trouble towns).
