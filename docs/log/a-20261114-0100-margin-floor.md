# A €15 margin floor turns the week positive

**What we saw.** On b2, driver fees ate the whole price: about €108 of revenue against €106 of fee per order.

**The idea.** Decline an option when its price minus the fee we will post (1.4x the typical fee) is under €15. List over typical fee runs from 0.94 to 1.50, so this keeps the lanes whose list price is high for their driving cost.

**The test.** dev-01..03, level 8, against b2 (the same, without a floor). b5 also tries a cheaper flat fee of 1.2x.

| Tag | dev-01 | dev-02 | dev-03 | On time | Ranked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| b2: no floor, fee 1.4x | -€900 (242) | -€5,001 (322) | -€1,518 (408) | 87.6-89.7% | 0/3 |
| b4: floor €15, fee 1.4x | €3,819 (201) | €2,940 (193) | €3,245 (253) | 87.4-90.5% | 1/3 |
| b5: floor €15, fee 1.2x | €4,763 (232) | €3,888 (259) | €2,030 (281) | 84.7-87.3% | 0/3 |

Late orders on b4 (`lab/b/late_why.py`): booked after `lastPickupAt` 11-19 per week, booked in time but still late 2-7, visit failed 2-3, drops 1-4. A gig posted with under 3 hours to `lastPickupAt` almost never books (dev-02 on b2: 1 of 36).

**Keep or kill.** Keep the floor: +€4k to +€8k a week over b2 with fewer orders. Kill the 1.2x fee for now: it gains margin but costs 2-3 points of on-time share, and the gate is the cliff. Defaults are now the b4 settings.
