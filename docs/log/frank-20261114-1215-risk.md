# A3: V2 Risk (`src/spreadbot/risk.py`)

## What we saw
Why won cars go late (`research/sessionA/scripts/a3_late_why.py`, one mechanism per late car):
- c3 (dev-07..10, 364 cars/wk): 21.5 late/wk. 12.5 were never booked before the world's 3 h deadline warning, 4.2 dropped and rebooked, 2.8 booked early but picked up late, 1.2 delivered late, 0.5 booked < 3 h before the last pickup.
- p7 (c3 + mu_floor 1.15 + load_free 0.12, 5 weeks, 395 cars/wk): 33.6 late/wk. Booked only after the warning 20.2 (+7.7), drop/rebook 6.8 (+2.6), others flat.
- The growth is in Friday deadlines (78 -> 105 cars/wk, late 9% -> 13%) and Mon2 (2% -> 8%). Cars without a warning stay 2% late in every run; warned cars are 66-77% late.
- Trade-ins are 46-55% late (3% of cars), private ends 15-21%, chains 4-8% (less than singles).
- p3/p5 (flat fee 1.25) on dev-07/08: same picture, 30-31 late/wk vs 19 for c3.

## The idea
P(late) of a won car from what the planner knows: slack, lead from the planned post (16:00 the day before the planned day) to the last
pickup, pickup on the deadline day, deadline weekday, drive, trade-in, private, note, premium, drivers within 80 km, our open unbooked gigs
in the region, chain, and the world-wide day load (cars due that day / drivers, with a convex term above 0.1).
A regional load (cars per Supply.hours) added nothing once the world-wide load is in (held-out coef -0.06), so Risk does not use it.

## The test
Logistic fit on 87,606 won cars (v1-evening + v1-a runs). Held-out seeds % 4 == 3 (23,288 cars). Runtime check: replays through a Desk
calling Risk.p_late at each win (`lab/a/risk_check.py`). Unit tests `tests/a/test_risk.py` (3).

## The numbers
Held-out AUC 0.807; OnTime's p_ok as quoted 0.727; OnTime's features refit 0.730.
Calibration by decile, pred/actual: 0.006/0.012, 0.010/0.009, 0.014/0.018, 0.018/0.012, 0.023/0.018, 0.029/0.030, 0.039/0.038, 0.054/0.061, 0.083/0.096, 0.255/0.255.
p_ok over-predicts lateness (0.023/0.018 ... 0.181/0.144).
Per run: c3 0.056 vs 0.059; p3 0.076 vs 0.071; p5 0.067 vs 0.078; p7 0.075 vs 0.087. The volume probes are under-predicted by 1-1.5 points: few played weeks reach that load.

## Keep or kill
Keep as V2's P(late) term. The lever is not admission alone: at volume, late cars are cars nobody booked in time, mostly due Friday, so
dispatch (Friday posting and fees) must change with volume.
