# Builder: fresh block dev-43..54, the hard week, and why the 92% guard never engaged

**What we saw.** On 12 seeds never used for tuning (dev-43..54), c1 (`--preset c1`) made €13,410/week, ranked 11/12. dev-43 is a
hard week for every config: c3 0.873 (€6,799), chains_plus 0.884 (€5,405), c1 0.892 (€8,189), y4 0.909 (€8,460, the only ranked one).
The governor's projection (new GOV log line, every 2 world hours) read 0.97 on Tuesday and 0.94 on Thursday noon, and fell under
0.92 only on Friday; the week ended at 0.892. In normal weeks the projection on Thursday noon is within a point of the final share.

**Why** (`lab/build/lateby.py`, `lab/build/openstate.py` on the reveals):
- Late cars are the ones no driver booked until < 4 h before their last pickup (24 of 33 such cars late on c1 dev-43).
- At any time of the week, an order whose gig is posted but unbooked ends late 40-55% of the time (13 weeks, Wed/Thu/Fri noon);
  booked orders 2-5% (14% on dev-43 Friday), orders still held for the release 1-5%. The projection counted the unbooked
  ones at their quote-time P(on time) (~0.85). dev-43 simply has more of them (22 at Thursday noon vs 2-18).
- Bulk batches: on dev-43 c1 won 15 of a 31-car batch from one yard; big-batch cars were 25% late vs 7.5% for the rest.

**Other measurements (offline, no worlds).**
- On-time model calibration (`lab/build/fit_late.py`, 20,551 won orders of c1/c3/c4/y4 runs): late / quoted P(late) is 0.55 under
  1 h of driving, 0.30-0.36 for 1-4 h, 0.47 for 4-6 h and 1.57 over 6 h. Own unbooked backlog within 30 km adds little
  (held-out AUC 0.750 -> 0.755); deadlines on Friday / last Monday are not later once drive time is known.
- Competitor learning: with only a slower option quoted, dealers accept 31% of the time vs 52% for the fastest option at the
  same ratio over our live mu (16,818 quotes), so those losses drag mu down.

**The ideas** (flags, all off by default): `gov_stuck_p` (projection counts an order unbooked for an hour as this likely on time),
`pl_cal` (risk price scaled by drive time), `learn_fastest`, `strict_warn`, `hold_weekend`, `repair_pairs`, `stop_windows`,
`fee_open_dense`; preset `c1_plus` (c1 + max_per_batch 6).
