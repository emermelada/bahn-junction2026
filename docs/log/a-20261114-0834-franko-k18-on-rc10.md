# Franko's v1 (preset chains / k18) on our fresh seeds: twice our margin

**What we saw.** Round "Saturday 00:00": the top ten teams make €15-17k a week on 373-458 orders; we made €7.5k on 254. Franko's branch (origin/branch-v1-frank @ 6030678) reports €11.3k a week on dev-07..18 and €11.5k on held-out dev-19..30 with preset `chains` (k18), ranked 24/24, and €9.7k vs our €6.1k on the same 12 weeks for his rc2.

**The idea.** Check his best preset on seeds he never saw: our rc10.

**The test.** His harness (`harness/sweep.py --tag jv-k18-rc10 --seeds rc10-01..rc10-12 --set ...` with the 12 settings of PRESETS["chains"]), run from a separate worktree (../frank-v1), against our rc5 on the same seeds.

| rc10 | Ranked | Min on-time | Mean | Median | Worst week | Orders a week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| our rc5 | 11/12 | 88.4% | €5,371 | €5,552 | €1,399 | 173 |
| Franko k18 | 12/12 | 90.1% | €10,791 | €10,797 | €6,731 | 361 |

He wins every week, including rc10-07 (our miss at 88.4%; his €6,731 at 92.6%). Weakest point: rc10-02 at 90.1% on time.

What his bot does that ours does not: prices each option by expected margin on a live competitor model (week level, today's shift, per-dealer shift); a logistic on-time model refit on 6,291 of his orders decides which cars to take, instead of hard declines; a per-day load price for capacity; fees opening at 1.05x with raises at 8 h and 5 h before the last pickup; holds won cars up to 8 h to form two-car chains (a two-car gig costs about 0.85x the typical fees against 1.14x for a single); a full notes reader.

**Keep or kill.** Keep his bot as the team's bot for the ranking rounds and the final, pending his confirmation of the preset and our live checks (final runner on Linux, chaos test).
