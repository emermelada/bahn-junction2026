# Fee by slack: a cheaper flat fee when the last pickup is a day away

**What we saw.** Driver fees were the largest cost: every gig opened at a flat 1.4x the typical fee. On b2, gigs posted a day or more before their last pickup booked in time 93% of the time (250 of 268).

**The idea.** Post those gigs at 1.2x. The early raise to 1.8x, 6 hours before the last pickup, catches any that stay open. The cost oracle plans the same fee, so the €15 margin floor admits more next_day orders.

**The test.** Same 9 rc1 seeds, paired against b16 (the defaults: b11 plus the notes reader).

| Tag | Ranked | Min on-time | p10 | Median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| b16 baseline | 9/9 | 94.6% | €1,178 | €3,876 |
| b15 long-slack fee 1.2x | 9/9 | 94.6% | €2,697 | €6,753 |

Every seed gains, from +€1,316 (rc1-07) to +€4,456 (rc1-04).

Fee ladder (lab-f1..f3, flat fees drawn in [0.8, 2.0], no raise), share of gigs booked before the last pickup:

| Fee / typical | 0.8-0.9 | 0.9-1.0 | 1.0-1.1 | 1.1-1.2 | 1.2-1.3 | 1.4-1.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a day or more of slack (n 28-41 per bin) | 86% | 97% | 100% | 100% | 100% | 96% |
| under 8 hours (n 5-14 per bin) | 22% | 50% | 71% | 67% | 80% | 62% |

Rescue direct offers (b17, 1.8x the typical fee after a deadline warning, to viewers and drivers living within 30 km): 305 offers on one week, none accepted (97 declined, 208 expired), no change in score. Removed.

**Keep or kill.** Keep: the 1.2x long-slack fee is the default. Next: 1.0x for long slack (b18), 1.3x for short slack (b19), a 1.2x tier for 8-24 hours (b20).
