# Quote ratio 1.45: same money, safer weeks

**What we saw.** On fresh seeds the rc4 defaults miss the gate in about 1 week in 12 (rc7-06: 82.0%, -€340). Every idea that added orders lost money; a slightly higher price takes fewer, better-paid orders.

**The idea.** `dealer.quote_ratio` 1.45 or 1.5 instead of 1.4, all else rc4.

**The test.** rc7-01..12 and rc8-01..12 (24 weeks), paired.

| Ratio | Ranked (24) | Min on-time | Worst week | Mean delta rc8 / rc7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.4 (rc4) | 23/24 | 82.0% | -€340 | |
| 1.45 | 24/24 | 91.3% | €1,627 | -€3 / -€68 |
| 1.5 | 23/24 | 88.8% | €1,294 | -€497 / -€90 |

At 1.45, rc7-06 goes from 82.0% and -€340 to ranked and €3,929. rc8-05, the worst rc8 week, falls from €2,146 to €1,627.

Confirmation on rc9-01..12:

| Ratio | Ranked | Min on-time | p10 | Median | Worst week | Mean delta |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.4 (rc4) | 10/12 | 79.3% | €3,317 | €6,324 | -€2,732 | |
| 1.45 | 11/12 | 84.4% | €2,503 | €6,247 | -€345 | +€175 |

Over the 36 fresh weeks: ranked 35 at 1.45 against 33 at 1.4, for about the same mean margin. rc9-05 fails at both (79.3% and 84.4%).

**Keep or kill.** Keep: 1.45 is the default, tag rc5.
