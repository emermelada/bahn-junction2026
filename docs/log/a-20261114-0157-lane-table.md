# Lane table: the competitor's lane factor, from 443 weeks

**What we saw.** At quotes of 1.3-1.75x list, win rates per lane run from 11% (p10) to 62% (p90) across our level 8 weeks. RULES: one part of the competitor's lane factor is almost the same in every world.

**The idea.** Fit P(win) = sigmoid(a_lane - 8 x ratio) on every level 8 quote we have made (303,778 quotes, 836 lanes from pickup town to delivery town). The slope 8 comes from the price ladder, where ratios were drawn at random inside one week; a slope fitted on the pooled weeks comes out at 1.8 because each week has its own competitor level. Each lane's offset is pulled towards the average with the weight of 30 quotes. The shift (a_lane - a0) / 8 is how much higher a ratio wins as often on that lane: p10 -0.30, median 0.00, p90 +0.25. Quoting adds `lane_weight` x shift (capped at ±0.35) to the ratio.

**The test.** The rc2 seeds are in the training data about 240 times, so the test uses 12 fresh seeds (rc4-01..12): rc3 defaults against lane_weight 0.5 and 1.0.
