# Investing in trust with big dealers: no gain

**What we saw.** Dealers are concentrated: on rc5 the top dealer sends 310-330 of about 1,035 requests a week, the second 130-170, the top ten 78%. Trust (RULES, level 8) lifts the prices a dealer accepts by 1% per on-time order, up to 25%, and we already scale prices by it.

**The idea.** Quote big dealers (10% or more of this week's requests) lower until their trust count is full, to reach +25% sooner.

**The test.** 12 fresh seeds rc6-01..12, rc4 defaults, `dealer.trust_invest` 0.1 and 0.2.

| Tag | Ranked | Min on-time | p10 | Median | Mean delta | Orders a week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rc6-base (rc4) | 11/12 | 89.6% | €4,422 | €5,723 | | 225 |
| trust_invest 0.1 | 11/12 | 88.0% | €3,842 | €6,224 | -€19 | 246 |
| trust_invest 0.2 | 10/12 | 86.9% | €2,835 | €4,644 | -€956 | 238 |

rc6-01 is the base's missed week (89.6%): 15 of 20 late orders were booked after their last pickup, Oulu 6 late of 13. Late share by drivers living within 30 km of the pickup, over all 12 base weeks: under 10 drivers 6.6%, 10-79 drivers 6-7%, 80 or more 3.0%; margin per order is the same in each band, so no simple town threshold pays.

**Keep or kill.** Kill. The extra wins at a lower price cost as much as the faster trust earns, and they use drivers.
