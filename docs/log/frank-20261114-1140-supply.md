# A3: V2 Supply (`src/spreadbot/supply.py`)

## What we saw
302 played level-8 weeks (v1 / v1-evening / v1-best reveals, <= 8 per seed; `research/sessionA/scripts/a3_supply_fit.py`, `a3_supply_who.py`):
- Planner-hours (drive + 30 min per car) our cars use per day: ~160-260 Tue-Fri, 100 Mon2, 34 Mon, 19 Sat; the capital is ~62%.
- Capital gigs are driven 82% by capital drivers; elsewhere only 30-40% by local drivers. The rest come from the capital
  (35-67% of each region's gigs, approach 2-6 h). Supply outside the capital is the capital's mobile pool, not local homes.
- No saturation in the range we play: in the capital, from 0.24 to 0.87 driver-hours per resident driver a day, book time
  (150-219 min) and drop share (11-15%) stay flat. Snow region-days show no fall in hours used (x1.10).
- Roster per region varies a lot between worlds (Jyvaskyla 3-276, Oulu 7-194 drivers): the base must scale with this roster.
- Morning (05-13 h) drops: 0-3 on 92% of weekdays, >= 5 on 4% (flu days).

## The idea
hours(region, day) = p95 Tue-Fri planner-hours of the region x 1.5 headroom x roster factor (share driven by residents x
this world's residents / mean + the rest) x day share (Mon, Mon2 = weekday; Sat 0.17; Sun 0) x learned (region's readily-booked
share vs the week's, shrunk by 30 bookings, 0.8-1.2) x flu (0.7 after 5 morning drops) x snow (0.9). 10 regions = driver pools.
Folded from events only (gig_booked, gig_dropped, forecast, gig_viewed for the log); same events give the same numbers.

## The test
Offline, held-out seeds (number % 4 == 3, 75 weeks; prior refit on the other 227): replay each week into Supply, read
hours(region, day) at 16:00 the day before, compare with planner-hours really used (`lab/a/supply_check.py`).
Unit tests `tests/a/test_supply.py` (5 tests: regions cover the map, >= 0, deterministic replay, weekend/flu/snow, learned, roster).

## The numbers
u = used / hours, Tue-Fri: capital p50 0.38, p95 0.62, max 1.05 (1.7% of days over 1); other regions p50 0.11-0.29, p95 0.44-0.99.
All region-days over 1.0: 0-4.7% (Tampere and Pori highest). Sat p95 0.72, Mon2 p95 0.55. corr(hours, used) 0.88.
Learned factor p5/p50/p95 0.91 / 1.00 / 1.05: regions differ little in how readily they book; it moves hours by a few %.
With the planner's lam_free 0.5, c3's typical capital day is free and its busiest 10% start to pay.

## Keep or kill
Keep as V2's capacity prior. Not tested in a world (needs the planner). Open: HEADROOM 1.5 is the one unmeasured number (no
saturation seen); the planner's lam is only as good as it. Views are folded but not used (they track our postings).
